How to play

  • Tap a card to move it to the best pile (or turn the stock). A tapped card in the middle of a pile moves the cards on top of it too.
  • Drag cards to move them yourself. Piles you can drop on light up.
  • Press and hold a card to peek at it when cards overlap.
  • Undo and Redo are unlimited. A game is saved automatically.
  • Every game follows its own rules; if a move is not allowed, the cards just slide back.
  • Install it: in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen. In Chrome use the menu → Install app. It then works offline.
